# Codex Badge v0.3.3

Capsule stability, display scaling, and reliable early-recovery fireworks.

## Changes

- Keep the verified avatar anchor during image viewing when only the navigation-column bounds become zero; retain the existing invalidation and recovery rules for other changes.
- Align WebView rendering with native DPI, size and center the capsule from the visible avatar geometry, and fix clipping in quota and reset-credit panels.
- Center details in compact sidebars and align them left in wider sidebars, using the card's existing padding.
- Correct update-panel sizing and dragging across display scales; show Chinese release summaries inside the app while keeping this release page in English.
- Save recent valid quota evidence per account for up to seven days, so an early weekly recovery can be detected after a restart. First readings without history only establish a baseline. Without reset-credit evidence, require at least a 10-point gain to 95% or more, at least five minutes before the previous scheduled reset, followed by a valid confirmation at least 60 seconds later. These local heuristics do not prove an official global reset.
- Keep confirmed effects per account for 24 hours, preserve them across welcome effects and account switches, and retry failed playback or saves. An already-open detail panel can play a newly confirmed effect. Events are consumed only after rendering starts and the playback acknowledgement is saved, with restart deduplication. Natural scheduled resets do not trigger fireworks.

## Validation and limits

146 Rust tests (5 interactive/manual tests excluded) and 49 frontend/installer tests passed on the final source; the signed-installer verification and tamper-rejection check also passed. Automated checks cover anchor geometry, DPI/layout, per-account recovery, restart/expiry, read interruption, failed saves and renderer acknowledgements, welcome/reset coexistence, deduplication, and update/installer behavior.

Earlier isolated image-viewing checks were accepted by the user, with 81 protected zero-column reads and no hidden capsule samples. Earlier DPI checks included simulated 100%-300% layouts and isolated native 125% scaling mismatch checks; these are prior-stage evidence, not a complete acceptance run of this release. Real multi-PC/mixed-DPI behavior, actual Pro recovery fireworks, and full install/upgrade/restart acceptance remain unverified. Historical readings absent from older releases cannot be reconstructed. No chameleon preview or unfinished animal features are included.
